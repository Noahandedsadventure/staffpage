Field Operations Protocols

Trackside Checklist

  • Verify scanner reception on 161.415 MHz prior to departure.
  • Ensure high-visibility vests and proper footwear are in vehicle.
  • Maintain minimum 15-25ft distance from active right-of-way at all times.
  • Log mileage location when spotting rare power or foreign units.

Media Pipeline Workflow

  • Offload raw SD card footage to storage immediately after field runs.
  • Label clips with date, location, and engine number (e.g., 2026-09-17_Piney_CN3851.mp4).
  • Pass raw cuts to Noah for color pass and edit assembly.
  • Sync with team prior to setting stream or upload live times.
